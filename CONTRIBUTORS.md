## How you get added

- **Merged pull request:** the maintainer adds you after the merge.
- **Anything else** (a good bug report, an idea that shipped, helping someone in an issue, a blog post): comment on
  any issue or PR with
  ```
  @all-contributors please add @your-github-name for bug, ideas
  ```
  and the bot opens a PR adding you. Use the words from the table: `code`, `doc`, `bug`, `test`, `ideas`, `review`,
  `translation`, `design`, `infra`, `example`, `security`, `talk`, `question`, `maintenance`.
- Forgot someone, or got missed yourself? Open an issue. We'd rather over-credit than under-credit.
