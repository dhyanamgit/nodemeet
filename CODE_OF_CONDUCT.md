# Code of conduct

nodemeet follows the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming and patient, especially with first-time contributors. Criticise code, never people. No
harassment, insults or personal attacks in any project space. Report problems to the maintainer privately through
GitHub. Maintainers may remove comments, lock threads or ban people who don't follow it.
